FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Admission FAQs

Q: Are initial counselling sessions free of charge?

Yes! Initial aptitude assessment, stream advice, and college comparison counselling are completely free for all students and parents.

Q: When should I submit my admission enquiry?

Right after your 12th board exams or as soon as exam results are announced. Early registration guarantees priority counselling and reserved management seats in premier autonomous colleges.

Q: Can Shasthi Guru help if I have a moderate or lower cutoff?

Absolutely. Every student has unique potential. We match students with emerging specialized colleges that have excellent placements in specialized domains like AI, robotics, biotechnology, and nursing.

LEGAL COMPLIANCE

Privacy & Student Data Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Shasthi Guru Dream Campus, we take the confidentiality of student educational and demographic records with the utmost gravity.

1. Sensitive Personal Information & Aadhaar Security

In accordance with national data privacy standards, Aadhaar card numbers are never mandated at initial enquiry stages. Where provided for official scholarship verification, numbers are encrypted in storage, masked on internal dashboards (•••• •••• XXXX), and never transmitted over plaintext channels or public messaging applications.

2. Purpose of Collection

Student marks, cutoff details, and contact numbers are collected exclusively to facilitate college choice-filling, admission quota counseling, and welfare grant disbursements.

3. Zero Third-Party Sale

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade student records to commercial telemarketing databases.
